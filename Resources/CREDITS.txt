MeetCallRecorder - image sources
================================

Everything this app puts on screen is either drawn by it or listed below.


Sample wallpapers
-----------------

Eight wallpapers ship inside the app. They are used in exactly one
place: the background preview in Settings > Background. They stand in
for the two photo modes so that opening the settings window never has
to download anything, and so the preview looks the same with the
network down. They are never used in a recording - a real call gets a
wallpaper fetched live (see below) or a background the app draws
itself.

All eight came from wallhaven.cc, out of the same pool the app itself
searches: general category, SFW. Each was cropped to 16:9, scaled to
640x360 - the size the preview actually draws them at - and re-encoded
as WebP at quality 35. The whole set is about 140 KB; the individual
files run 10-31 KB against originals of 0.4-4.3 MB. Follow the links
for the full-size files and for whatever terms the uploader attached
to them.

Wallpapers downloaded while running
-----------------------------------

The "Random photo" and "Photo per meeting" background modes fetch a
wallpaper from the wallhaven.cc API when a call starts, and cache it
under ~/Library/Caches/MeetCallRecorder/backgrounds. Searches are
restricted to the general category at SFW purity, 16:9 and at least
1920x1080, sorted by the past year's toplist.

  API:   https://wallhaven.cc/help/api
  Terms: https://wallhaven.cc/terms

Those images belong to their respective uploaders and are not
redistributed by this app - they live in a local cache on the machine
that downloaded them, and end up only in that machine's own
recordings.


Code
----

  DynamicColor - https://github.com/yannickl/DynamicColor
    MIT licence, (c) Yannick Loriot. Colour manipulation used by the
    "Gradient per meeting" background: harmonies, tints and shades, and
    palette interpolation in Cie L*a*b*, which is what keeps the midpoint
    between two distant hues a colour instead of grey.


Everything else
---------------

  Gradients, title cards, the welcome screen's mark
    Drawn at runtime by the app (BackgroundArtwork, TitleCardRenderer,
    WelcomeIconMark). No third-party artwork; the gradient's colour
    maths uses DynamicColor, above.

  Desktop picture background
    Whatever the Mac's own desktop picture is at the time.

  Google Meet and Slack icons
    Read off the installed applications on this Mac at launch, never
    bundled - see AppIcons. They belong to Google and Slack
    respectively and are shown only to label their own rows.

  Everything else in the interface
    SF Symbols, from macOS.
